## Design Context

### Users
The league manager uses this local companion beside CBS during a live 16-team keeper draft. Record picks quickly, then assess the next move without losing context.

### Brand Personality
Confident, focused, sporting. Help the user feel in control under draft-clock pressure.

### Aesthetic Direction
User-selected sports editorial: warm paper, forest green, bold scoreboard typography, compact readable rows. Barlow Condensed display paired with Source Sans 3 body. No glowing dashboards or walls of scoring formulas.

### Design Principles
- Player search and recording are primary; support keyboard and touch.
- Distinguish the on-clock team from recommendations for the user's roster.
- Show concise guidance first, scoring details on demand.
- Keep roster, keepers, and history inspectable.
- Confirm mutations and report failures; never imply automatic CBS integration or saved sessions.
